## what i'm working on right now

- a voice assistant for my car, on a raspberry pi 5. it's on my bench, not in the car yet — wake word in, spoken answer out. i'll put the code up when the loop actually works end to end.
- a little radar tracker: an esp32 reading an HLK-LD2450 mmWave sensor. i wrote the parser that turns its serial output into live target positions, and it tracks me walking around the room. a screen is next.

i work nights on an amazon robotics floor, and i'm studying logic and discrete math on the side. the plan is to move into it support / mechatronics work — this page is basically my evidence folder for that.

## the repos

- **BirdWatch** — a camera feed that flags people, vehicles, and unattended bags with YOLOv8, streams to a flask dashboard, and emails me when something shows up.
- **World-Trade-Center-Simulation** — a physics model where every input is sourced or flagged as an estimate. i wanted to see what the numbers actually say when you do them yourself.
- the rest are private — my planning system and a couple of business experiments. happy to walk through any of it if you ask.
