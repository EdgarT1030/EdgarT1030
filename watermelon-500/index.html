🍉 Watermelon 500

Snap a photo, tap the rind, know before you cut.

📸 Photo check

Take a photo of the whole melon, including its "field spot" (the patch that rested on the ground).

🎤 Tap-sound check

Hold your phone ~15cm from the rind, then firmly knuckle-tap the melon 3–4 times during the countdown.

Verdict

    Heuristic estimate only, based on skin colour and tap-tone analysis — not a lab measurement. Combine with the classic checks: dried curly tendril at the stem, matte (not shiny) skin, and a melon that feels heavy for its size.
